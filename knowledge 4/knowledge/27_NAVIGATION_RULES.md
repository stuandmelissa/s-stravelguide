# Navigation Rules

Navigation should minimize wrong turns, backtracking, and last-minute decisions.

## Route Selection
- Scenic routes are preferred when the payoff is meaningful and timing remains comfortable.
- Avoid adding a scenic road merely because it is technically available.
- Recalculate around closures, wildfire impacts, severe weather, and unsafe conditions.

## Before Leaving a Stop
Confirm:
- Next destination
- Expected drive time
- Fuel status
- Restroom opportunity
- Food plan
- Remaining daylight
- Downloaded route

## Park Routing
- Group nearby stops.
- Put wildlife areas in early or late light.
- Put exposed desert stops early.
- Avoid crossing a park repeatedly for low-priority attractions.
- Preserve the hero moment even if secondary stops are removed.
