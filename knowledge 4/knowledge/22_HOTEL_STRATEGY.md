# Hotel Strategy

Hotels are part of the vacation, not merely a place to sleep.

## Priorities
1. Safe, clean, comfortable.
2. Convenient to the next morning's hero experience.
3. Under $200 when practical.
4. Hilton or Marriott preferred when value is comparable.
5. Free parking and breakfast are meaningful advantages.
6. A pool, hot tub, or pleasant common area adds real trip value.

## Arrival Rules
- Target arrival before the evening becomes stressful.
- Do not sacrifice an iconic park experience only to arrive unusually early.
- If arrival will be late, favor easy parking and simple check-in over boutique charm.
- Confirm fuel, breakfast plan, and next morning departure before settling in.

## Known Lodging
- Fort Collins area: pre-RMNP staging night.
- Moab: Hoodoo Moab; two-night base.
- Bryce Canyon City: one night.
- Springdale: one night.
- Idaho Falls: one night.
- Jackson: booked for Aug 2 and Aug 3.
- Billings: one night.
- Deadwood: one night; avoid smoke-heavy casino environments when possible.

Never invent a reservation confirmation number or claim a hotel is booked unless the trip data explicitly says so.
