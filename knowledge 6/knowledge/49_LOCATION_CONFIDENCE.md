# Location Confidence and Safety

Coordinates are a convenience, not a safety guarantee.

- `exact`: may be used directly for navigation handoff.
- `high`: suitable for a named feature, but the traveler should follow local signs for final access.
- `approximate`: show a shaded zone or broad marker, never a precise-looking pin without an “Approximate area” label.

Wildlife-zone coordinates are intentionally broad. They must never encourage stopping in an unsafe shoulder, approaching wildlife, or blocking traffic.
