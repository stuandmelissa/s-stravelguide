# Scenic Drive Layers

Pack 6 includes separate overlays for:

- Trail Ridge Road
- Utah Scenic Byway 12 corridor
- Grand Teton scenic loop
- Yellowstone highlights circuit
- Beartooth Highway

A scenic-drive layer should display named pull-offs, priority tiers, approximate duration, and a clear turnaround/exit point. Do not crowd the map with every possible turnout; default to hero and core stops, with bonus stops hidden behind an expand control.
