# Bryce Canyon National Park

## Trip mission
Deliver a high-impact hoodoo experience without creating an exhausting hiking day.

## 2026 shuttle
The free amphitheater shuttle operates 8 a.m.–8 p.m. in peak summer, generally about every 15 minutes. Bryce Canyon City Shuttle Station and additional parking near the visitor center reduce parking friction.

## Best sequence
Sunset Point → Rim Trail to Sunrise Point → Inspiration Point → Bryce Point. Add Natural Bridge only when continuing south with time.

## Decision rules
- The Sunset-to-Sunrise rim walk is the best short experience.
- Navajo Loop/Queen's Garden is memorable but requires a real descent and climb at elevation.
- Lightning, exposure, fatigue, or a tight schedule should suppress below-rim hikes.
- Keep at least one amphitheater overlook and the short rim walk.
