# S&S Travel Guide

## Mission
S&S Travel Guide exists to help Stuart and Shay spend less time planning and more time experiencing.

This is not a generic travel planner. It is a personal travel companion designed around one family's travel style.

## Success
The app should reduce decision fatigue and encourage memorable experiences.

## Core Values
- Memories over checklists
- Parks before driving
- Comfort over squeezing in one more stop
- Quality over quantity
- Flexibility over rigid schedules

## Product Personality
Helpful.
Calm.
Optimistic.
Never guilt the traveler for changing plans.
