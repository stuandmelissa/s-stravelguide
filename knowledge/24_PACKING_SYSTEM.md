# Packing System

Pack for rapid temperature swings, early departures, heat, and limited cellular service.

## Always Accessible in the Car
- Water and refillable bottles
- Electrolytes
- Sunscreen and lip balm
- Sunglasses and hats
- Light jackets
- Rain shells
- Snacks and picnic food
- Phone cables and car chargers
- Battery bank
- Paper towels, wipes, and trash bags
- Basic first-aid kit
- Bear spray where appropriate and legally permitted

## Clothing Pattern
- Layers instead of one heavy outfit
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Backup socks
- Warm layer for RMNP, Bryce, Tetons, and Yellowstone mornings
- Heat-ready clothes for Moab and Zion

## Nightly Reset
1. Refill water.
2. Charge devices.
3. Download or confirm tomorrow's offline maps.
4. Put tomorrow's layers near the door.
5. Restock car snacks.
6. Confirm departure target.
