# Emergency Playbook

The emergency screen must be usable with one hand and without service.

## Immediate Emergency
Call 911 when available.

## Screen Content
- Current location coordinates
- Nearest known ranger station or visitor center
- Nearest hospital or urgent care if cached
- Vehicle description
- Emergency contacts
- Insurance and roadside-assistance details when supplied

## Common Problems
### Vehicle issue
Move to a safe location, use hazard lights, conserve phone battery, and contact roadside assistance.

### Lost in a park
Stop moving, stay visible, conserve battery, use downloaded maps, and contact rangers or 911 when possible.

### Severe weather
Follow official closures and warnings. Do not drive through flooded roads or continue onto exposed high-elevation roads during dangerous conditions.

### Missing person
Record last known location, time, clothing, direction of travel, and phone status; contact park rangers immediately.

Do not overload this screen with general travel content.
