# Grand Teton National Park

## Trip mission
Build a north-to-south scenic day with reflections, mountain foregrounds, and realistic wildlife opportunities before Jackson.

## 2026 operations
Moose-Wilson Road is open through but can have delays of 45 minutes or longer through Labor Day. A section remains gravel; stopping is prohibited in the construction zone. Death Canyon Road is closed. Jenny Lake parking is commonly very busy from about 9 a.m.–3 p.m.

## Best sequence
Oxbow Bend → Signal Mountain/Jackson Lake views → Jenny or String Lake → Cathedral Group Turnout → Teton Glacier Turnout → Chapel of the Transfiguration → Mormon Row if light/time justify it.

## Decision rules
- Jenny Lake before 9 a.m.; swap to String Lake rather than circle endlessly.
- Moose-Wilson is a wildlife opportunity, not a guaranteed sighting.
- Skip Moose-Wilson when delays threaten Jackson arrival.
- Preserve the mountain-view corridor and one meaningful lakeside stop.
