# Zion National Park

## Trip mission
Experience Zion Canyon efficiently from Springdale without losing the day to parking and shuttle queues.

## 2026 shuttle
The shuttle requires no ticket or reservation. In peak summer, the first canyon shuttle leaves at 7 a.m.; the last leaves the Visitor Center at 7 p.m.; the last outbound shuttle from Temple of Sinawava is 8:15 p.m.

## Best sequence
Board early → Temple of Sinawava/Riverside Walk → Big Bend → Zion Lodge for shade/food → optional Court of the Patriarchs.

## Decision rules
- Riverside Walk is the default short canyon hike; reaching the Narrows gateway does not obligate entering the river.
- Always surface flash-flood, heat, and closure checks.
- Canyon Overlook is a separate east-side option with limited parking; never recommend unsafe roadside parking.
- Preserve Big Bend and one canyon walk even when compressing.
