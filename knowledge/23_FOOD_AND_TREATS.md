# Food and Treats

Food should support the day, not consume it.

## Breakfast
- Early park mornings: quick hotel breakfast, yogurt, fruit, breakfast sandwich, or packed food.
- Avoid a long sit-down breakfast before a sunrise or wildlife window.

## Lunch
- Prefer flexible lunches after the morning hero experience.
- Use picnic food when restaurants would create backtracking or long waits.
- In hot parks, prioritize hydration and a shaded break.

## Dinner
- Prefer a good local restaurant when arrival time and energy allow.
- Chains are acceptable when everyone is tired or options are limited.
- Do not turn dinner selection into a stressful research project.

## Shay-Friendly Defaults
Chicken strips, burgers, pizza, pasta, fries, ice cream.

## Sweet Treat Rule
A worthwhile ice cream, bakery, shake, soda, or dessert stop is part of the itinerary—not a guilty detour.
