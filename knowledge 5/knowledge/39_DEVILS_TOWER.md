# Devils Tower National Monument

## Trip mission
Use Devils Tower as a high-payoff break between Billings and Deadwood.

## 2026 operations
Roads and trails are open 24 hours. Summer Visitor Center hours are 9 a.m.–6 p.m. through September 7. Parking is limited during summer.

## Best sequence
Safe approach-road photo → Visitor Center/base area → short Tower Trail segment or full 1.3-mile loop → prairie dog town.

## Decision rules
- A partial trail still delivers scale and texture.
- Leave exposed areas when lightning threatens.
- Observe prairie dogs from designated areas and never feed wildlife.
- Respect the site's cultural and spiritual significance.
