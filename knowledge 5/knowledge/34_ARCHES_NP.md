# Arches National Park

## Trip mission
See the most distinctive formations before heat and parking friction dominate.

## 2026 access
No advanced timed-entry reservation is required in 2026. Entry may still be temporarily diverted during congestion. Fiery Furnace experiences require separate reservations.

## Best sequence
Park Avenue viewpoint → Balanced Rock → Windows/Turret Arch → Double Arch → Delicate Arch viewpoints. Add Landscape Arch only when temperature, time, parking, and energy are favorable.

## Decision rules
- Windows + Double Arch is the highest scenery-per-minute cluster.
- Default to Delicate Arch viewpoints rather than the exposed full hike in summer.
- Convert hikes to viewpoints as temperatures rise.
- Skip order: Devils Garden extension, full Delicate Arch hike, Sand Dune Arch, then Park Avenue walk while retaining its viewpoint.
