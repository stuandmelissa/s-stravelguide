# Yellowstone National Park

## Trip mission
Deliver a compressed greatest-hits route from Jackson to Billings via Lamar Valley and Beartooth Highway.

## Priority stack
Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone; Old Faithful; Grand Prismatic; Lamar Valley; Mud Volcano when route/time fit.

## Route logic
South Entrance → Old Faithful → Grand Prismatic → Canyon → Tower/Lamar → Northeast Entrance → Cooke City → Beartooth Highway.

## 2026 operations
Check the official live road map. Canyon Village–West Thumb weekday work can cause repeated delays through August 14. Old Faithful area work can add delays. Beartooth Highway is projected open through October 12, weather permitting.

## Decision rules
- Never promise exact geyser timing without official live predictions.
- Wildlife jams can add substantial time; stop only when safely and legally parked.
- When behind, protect Canyon, one major thermal experience, Lamar, and Beartooth.
- Do not race severe weather or darkness on Beartooth.
