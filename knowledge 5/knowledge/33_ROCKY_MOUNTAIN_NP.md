# Rocky Mountain National Park

## Trip mission
Enter early from the east, complete one high-value Bear Lake experience, cross Trail Ridge Road, stop at Alpine Visitor Center when open, scan Kawuneeche Valley for moose, and exit at Grand Lake around noon–2 p.m.

## 2026 access
Bear Lake Road timed entry applies 5 a.m.–6 p.m.; rest-of-park timed entry, including Trail Ridge Road, applies 9 a.m.–2 p.m. Entering before the relevant window avoids the reservation requirement but does not guarantee parking.

## Best sequence
Bear Lake → Dream Lake, with Emerald Lake optional → Forest Canyon Overlook → one tundra pullout → Alpine Visitor Center → Kawuneeche Valley → Grand Lake.

## Decision rules
- Emerald Lake is worthwhile only with an early trail start and comfortable energy.
- Dream Lake is a successful endpoint if timing tightens.
- Sprague Lake or Moraine Park is the fallback when Bear Lake access fails.
- Protect the westbound crossing; do not spend the morning circling parking lots.
- Moose opportunities are strongest in wet meadows on the west side, never guaranteed.
